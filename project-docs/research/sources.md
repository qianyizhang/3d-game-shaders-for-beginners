# Asset source catalog

The entries below are candidate inputs, selected for a specific part of the
concept art. **Listed** means the publisher's page exposes the asset or
download and license. It does not mean the archive was opened or the asset
was rendered in the lab. **Lead** needs a license and import check.
**Reference** is for shape, staging or material study.

## Surfaces

[Poly Haven's asset license](https://polyhaven.com/license) and
[ambientCG's asset license](https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/) list their
downloadable assets as **CC0**. Use the actual asset download; treat listing
renders and collection images separately.

For an attribution license, record the author, asset and original link,
exact license/version and changes, then include attribution with the project
as required by that license. Verify the license in the downloaded archive or
live listing before accepting a lead. Free download status alone does not
settle redistribution. Keep an asset receipt at acquisition even for CC0.

[Blender's glTF export guide](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/addons/scene_gltf2.html)
describes material and animation export. Use a consistent Y-up handoff;
check indexed triangles, normals and tangent basis, map orientation and
color spaces, plus local pivots. Export only the resources needed by the lab.
The [original mill license](../../README.md#license) covers the C++ and shader
source; original mill art and audio are local reference material.
