# Single-scene bills of materials

[Compare all candidates](README.md) · [Sources and licenses](sources.md)

**Use** means a close fit with light cleanup. **Adapt** means reuse a source,
then change the mesh, material, UVs, or staging. **Make** means author the
feature to fit the lesson. Hours are hands-on art estimates; the final row
of each BOM includes scene assembly and a look pass.

## Canal weather station

[Concept board](../../.local/mill-scene-concepts/index.html#weather-station)
· **15–27 h** · Easiest path to a coherent replacement.

| BOM | Route and likely refinement | Art h |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 1 station shell, 1 sloped roof, 1 window recess | **Make.** Box and bevel construction; model the major roof edge, exposed ribs, window trim, copper gutter and a short flue. | 4–7 |
| 3 surfaces: wall, roof, timber | **Adapt.** [Sandstone](https://polyhaven.com/a/white_sandstone_bricks), [corrugated iron](https://polyhaven.com/a/corrugated_iron_02), [planks](https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_planks). Tint wall teal and roof rust-orange; set UV scale and normal strength by shot. | 2–4 |
| 1 channel, 1 dock, 2–3 steps, 1 patterned bed, 1 water mesh | **Make.** A few deep planes and modules. Shape two depth transitions so foam and refraction show. | 3–5 |
| 1 striped gauge, 1 beacon, 1 rigid weather prop | **Make.** A marked strip with strong color above and below water, a geometric lamp, and an axle or vane that can play the wheel’s teaching role. Use a static windsock for silhouette if needed. | 2–4 |
| 1–3 background props | **Use / adapt.** [Metal Toolbox](https://polyhaven.com/a/metal_toolbox) and [Ultimate Nature](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatenature.html); use a far-bank silhouette rather than a second neighborhood. | 1–2 |
| Shot setup and material cleanup | **Assemble.** Make the dry gauge, submerged mark and roof edge easy to find; frame a distant bank and isolate the beacon at night. | 3–5 |

**Match the board:** spend on the teal/orange contrast, gauge placement and
the luminous window. The keeper is optional for this scene; a posed keeper
is budgeted in the [workroom journey](journeys-and-effects.md#workroom-porch-canal).
Moving wetness, cloud shadows, and cloth are later exercises.

## Lantern ferry landing

[Concept board](../../.local/mill-scene-concepts/index.html#ferry-landing)
· **18–37 h** · A strong silhouette with two assets worth making.

| BOM | Route and likely refinement | Art h |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 1 stone quay with steps and opaque submerged paving | **Make / adapt.** Cut three or four stair shapes; use [Sandstone](https://polyhaven.com/a/white_sandstone_bricks) and a few actual ledge breaks. Keep near paving recognizable below water. | 3–6 |
| 1 river pavilion facade with curved eaves | **Make.** Repeat one tile strip across a roof patch; custom curved end silhouette and timber brackets. The rest can be a few flat surfaces. | 5–10 |
| 1 covered ferry, 1 bamboo pole, 1 water mesh | **Adapt or make.** [Kenney Watercraft](https://kenney.nl/assets/watercraft-kit) or [Quaternius Ships](https://quaternius.com/packs/ships.html) for blocking. Build or heavily adapt the shallow hull, canopy ribs and frame against the [Canton slipper-boat reference](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co41906/model-of-a-canton-slipper-boat-model-representation-boat). Make the pole with a readable end and water intersection. | 4–8 |
| 1 paper lantern, bracket and small brazier | **Make.** Lathe a shade, reuse the ribs, assign a separate bright interior; use a shallow metal bowl as the smoke source. [Download lead](sources.md#conditional-leads) is optional. | 1–3 |
| 2–3 distant ridge silhouettes, 1 accent tree | **Make / adapt.** Simple meshes or painted silhouettes, with separation in depth; one palette matched foliage asset. [The Met reference](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/51662) shows the composition opportunity. | 2–4 |
| Shot setup and look pass | **Assemble.** Show the lantern on dark wood and in water, then aim away; keep the dock and far ridges at distinct depths. | 3–6 |

**Match the board:** the boat canopy, upturned roof and warm lantern are
worth more than extra buildings. The baseline can use an empty tied ferry
with a fixed pole. Add **5–10 h** for a single posed ferryman if wanted
here; that pose is already part of the storm-journey character allowance,
so count it only once. The ink wash treatment is optional.

## Glasshouse and koi pond

[Concept board](../../.local/mill-scene-concepts/index.html#glasshouse)
· **18–35 h** · Most of the plants can be sourced; the frame is the work.

| BOM | Route and likely refinement | Art h |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 1 repeatable iron bay, 1 arched door, 1 side/end panel | **Make.** Model and mirror a few ribs and connectors using the [conservatory reference](https://www.nybg.org/planttalk/beginning-the-haupt-conservatorys-palm-dome-restoration/). Keep panes separate and use [Green Metal Rust](https://polyhaven.com/a/green_metal_rust) sparingly. A [free greenhouse lead](sources.md#conditional-leads) needs a license and mesh check. | 6–12 |
| 1 shallow pond, 1 step, 1 leaf stem at waterline, 1 water mesh | **Make / adapt.** Keep crisp tile geometry at the lip and a large authored checker over [tile detail](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Tiles002) on the bed. | 3–5 |
| 2–4 pots, 1 hero leaf, a few background leaves | **Use / adapt.** [Planter Pot Clay](https://polyhaven.com/a/planter_pot_clay) and [Calathea](https://polyhaven.com/a/calathea_orbifolia_01). Recolor one pot with a glaze, keep leaf cards clean at the edges. | 2–4 |
| 2 koi variants from 1 base | **Adapt.** [Quaternius’s CC0 koi GLB](https://poly.pizza/m/qyGtRmhgzl), previewed in the visual report. Adjust eye scale and color patches at pond viewing distance; export two still poses for the first build. | 2–5 |
| 1 warm fixture and stove vent | **Make.** Simple lamp and bright bulb against dark framing; add a small stovepipe for smoke. | 1–2 |
| Shot setup and material cleanup | **Assemble.** Show iron, ceramic and one lit leaf side by side, cast a strong frame shadow and leave the checker clear under water. | 4–7 |

**Match the board:** build a corner with three repeated bays, then compose
the pond shot. The estimate includes pane geometry and a still art pass.
Actual glass over water, leaf transmission, animated koi and caustics
need renderer work. Keep glass out of the baseline water ray path until
that layering is supported.

## Night market repair stall

[Concept board](../../.local/mill-scene-concepts/index.html#night-market)
· **17–32 h** · Strongest ready-made asset fit.

| BOM | Route and likely refinement | Art h |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 1 corner facade, 1 shutter and 1 visible alley block | **Adapt.** Start with [Downtown City MegaKit](https://quaternius.com/packs/downtowncitymegakit.html) free Standard subset. Rework the foreground bay and tailor a teal [ribbed metal shutter](https://polyhaven.com/a/green_metal_rust). Use kit geometry farther back. | 3–6 |
| 1 stall counter, 1 awning, 1–3 tool props | **Use / make.** [Metal Tool Chest](https://polyhaven.com/a/metal_tool_chest) is a close visual fit. Resize maps, leave drawers shut, build a simple counter and a folded ochre awning. | 3–5 |
| 1 curb, 1 recessed drain, 1 iron grate, 1 water mesh | **Make / adapt.** Stage a grate with a visible dry and wet half. Use [Asphalt](https://polyhaven.com/a/worn_asphalt) outside the basin and tile detail inside. Keep the water separate from wet pavement. | 4–7 |
| 1 geometric neon sign, 1 amber task lamp | **Make.** Simple tube or strip mesh and a broad source. Position them so a reflection can leave the frame. | 2–4 |
| 2–4 background utility props | **Adapt.** [Cyberpunk Game Kit](https://quaternius.com/packs/cyberpunkgamekit.html) and city subset as donors. Use only subdued shapes that don't fight the sign; make a small vent or cooking pot for the plume. | 2–4 |
| Shot setup and look pass | **Assemble.** Keep near curb, middle stall and far alley legible. Add a daytime or task-light shot for normal maps and shadows; avoid grading the basin to black. | 3–6 |

**Match the board:** one lamp, one neon mark and one polished foreground
prop carry the frame. Worn asphalt is a texture; uniform rain wetness and
opaque pavement reflections are separate from the lab's water reflections.

## Water puppet maker’s yard

[Concept board](../../.local/mill-scene-concepts/index.html#puppet-yard)
· **27–50 h** · Budget the custom creature first.

| BOM | Route and likely refinement | Art h |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 1 workbench stage, 1 frame/backdrop, 1 courtyard edge | **Adapt / make.** Use [Wooden Table 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/wooden_table_02) as a base, build a timber frame and paint the cloth motif. | 4–7 |
| 1 tiled pool, 1 rim, 1 submerged step, 1 water mesh | **Make.** Shape a shallow pool and keep room for the dragon tail and its reflected image. | 3–5 |
| 3 material treatments, 1 stage lamp and 1 small smoke vessel | **Adapt / make.** Use [Wood Planks](https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_planks), simple authored lacquer/metal masks, and a warm lamp against a dim curtain. Add a shallow vessel or chimney to locate smoke. | 2–4 |
| 2–4 craft props | **Use / make.** A [clay pot](https://polyhaven.com/a/planter_pot_clay), paint swatches, spare block and simple carving tools; only one needs close detail. | 2–3 |
| 1 lacquered dragon: head, body segments, tail | **Make.** Use the [animated dragon pack](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemonsters.html) only for scale or posing tests. The final face, painted profile and articulated tail define the scene. Reference the [water puppetry workshops](https://vietnam.travel/things-to-do/vietnam-water-puppets/). | 10–20 |
| Pivot placement and two static poses | **Make.** Model purposeful joints with local pivots and one repeatable tail-crossing pose. This is enough for a rigid comparison. | 2–4 |
| Shot setup and look pass | **Assemble.** Keep head, gold/teal highlights and tail silhouette readable without a craftsperson in frame. | 4–7 |

**Match the board:** make the dragon unmistakably lacquered wood, not a
generic monster with a tint. Stop at a few movable sections for the base.
A smooth rig, working pose editor and full human maker are extensions.
